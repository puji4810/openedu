# StudyOS measures its own Interventions

[0001](./0001-study-os-derives-competency-from-evidence.md) holds the Learner's capability to evidence, but StudyOS exempted its own recommendations from the same standard: an Intervention was scored by hand-written tables, accepted by the Learner, and never checked against what happened next. The scoring constants were therefore unfalsifiable — no observation could correct them. `study_coach(action="evaluate_interventions")` compares each accepted Intervention's recorded verification status against the evidence that arrived after its decision.

Outcomes are derived, not stored. The anchor is already durable — an accepted Plan Proposal carries `decided_at` and the status each Intervention was reasoning about — so an outcome is recomputed from attempts like every other StudyOS judgment and cannot drift out of sync with the evidence it summarises. Nothing new is written, and a corrected attempt corrects the outcome with it.

Two distinctions keep the result honest. An accepted Intervention that produced no later evidence is reported as `not_attempted` and excluded from the improvement rate entirely, because folding non-adherence into effectiveness would calibrate away sound advice that was simply never followed; adherence is reported separately. And below a minimum sample the aggregate withholds a rate and says `insufficient_evidence` rather than publishing a number a handful of decisions cannot support — presenting thin aggregates as verdicts is the failure this module exists to correct, and it would be absurd to commit it here.
